PRODUCT EXPERIENCE MATURITY

The public preview is the showroom. The connected application is the working system.

Storefront and Forge now have two deliberately separate deployment profiles. Public previews demonstrate the operating model with synthetic, bounded data. Connected customer-hosted profiles add authenticated product-to-product transport while preserving the human decision boundary. Operational status requires customer-selected validation and evidence.

PREVIEW

Demonstrate safely

Public Storefront and Forge experiences remain isolated from customer credentials, infrastructure authority and live provisioning. They explain the product journey without pretending to be the deployed customer system.

PORTFOLIO EVOLUTION

Eight layers from demonstration to bounded autonomous operations.

EP-16 is the current delivery focus while EP-08 remains the active portability track. GHE-07 is externally blocked on an authorized live target, so non-live-target engineering continues without pretending that gate is complete. Later layers do not inherit authority just because an earlier layer is complete.

01 · ACCEPTED BASELINE

Demonstrate the operating model

Public product previews and synthetic portfolio journeys establish the user experience and bounded product contracts.

PREVIEW→UNDERSTAND THE MODEL
02 · ACTIVE

Establish verifiable trust

Guard integrity, trusted profile approval/versioning, assessor evidence and restricted-network portability provide the trust foundation.

PROFILE→EVIDENCE→VERIFICATION
03 · NEXT

Connect the product system

Connected Storefront and Forge add authenticated handoff, provenance visibility and the explicit human-review stop without provisioning authority.

STORE→FORGE→REVIEW
04 · UPCOMING

Connect customer infrastructure

Customer identity, GitHub/GHES, runners, private CA/proxy/mirrors, durable audit and replaceable Crossplane/provider adapters are validated independently.

IDENTITY→REPOSITORY→EXECUTOR
05 · DOCUMENTATION-FIRST

Isolate every customer boundary

Customer, organization and environment context is carried through identity, trusted profiles, evidence, approvals, products, secrets, integrations and audit history. Cross-customer substitution must fail closed before any tenant-isolation claim.

CUSTOMER SCOPE→BOUND EVIDENCE→FAIL-CLOSED ISOLATION
06 · GATED

Prove it with a customer

A customer-selected control profile, permitted operational evidence and a real infrastructure product establish the scope for an explicit operational decision.

CUSTOMER PROFILE→REAL EVIDENCE→BOUNDED OPERATION
07 · FUTURE

Amplify with AI

AI can propose requirements, mappings, product configurations and explanations against versioned trusted inputs. Human acceptance remains accountable and verifiable.

PROPOSE→VALIDATE→HUMAN ACCEPT
08 · FUTURE / CONDITIONAL

Agent-operated infrastructure

Agents may eventually perform only explicitly authorized actions under least privilege, deterministic guardrails, retained evidence, rollback and human escalation.

INTENT→POLICY→BOUNDED ACTION
