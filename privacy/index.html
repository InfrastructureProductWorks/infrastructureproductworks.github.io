PRIVACY

Collect less. Expose less.

This portfolio presents product information without requiring a visitor account. The optional portfolio inquiry form asks for your name, email address, product, request type, summary and description. You may also provide reproduction steps and browser or environment details. Inquiry fields are submitted to a hosted processing endpoint; this is separate from simply browsing the website. Please provide only what is needed to explain your portfolio question. Do not include credentials, tokens, private keys, customer records or sensitive infrastructure details. The inquiry channel is not production support and does not authorize operational work.

Optional inquiries

Inquiry notice version: inquiry-2026-10-29. The form asks you to request processing for inquiry review and response. Leaving that choice unchecked does not prevent browsing this portfolio. It does not enroll you in marketing or grant permission to operate your infrastructure.

When inquiry intake is enabled, your submitted fields are sent to a receiver hosted by Render and recorded in a private GitHub issue for inquiry handling. Authorized operators with access to that issue can review the information and respond using the contact address you supply. The record includes the inquiry purpose, notice version and server receipt time. This records the submitted request; it does not verify the sender's identity.

Hosting, network security and service providers may process request metadata such as IP address, timestamp and browser information under their own service arrangements. The inquiry processing choice does not control essential network or security processing and does not authorize optional analytics, advertising or AI training.

For questions about an inquiry record, retention, access, correction or deletion, contact office@InfrastructureProductWorks.com. Include your inquiry reference if available, without resending sensitive material. We may need to verify that a request concerns your information; some records may need to be retained for applicable legal or security reasons. Please do not send identity documents unless a secure method has been arranged.

Installed applications and customer-hosted products require their own scope-specific data-handling review; this website notice does not establish a processing agreement for those products.

IaaP Guard data boundaries

Guard is designed around customer-controlled infrastructure workflows. Customers should not place credentials, personal data or unrelated sensitive information into support requests or public repositories. Product-specific data handling and permissions should be evaluated against the installed release and its documented configuration.
