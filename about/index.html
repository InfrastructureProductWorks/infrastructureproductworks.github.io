WHY INFRASTRUCTURE PRODUCT WORKS

Built from the gap between cloud infrastructure and product engineering.

Infrastructure Product Works is an independent portfolio exploring a simple proposition: enterprises should be able to consume governed infrastructure as durable products rather than repeatedly rebuilding architecture through tickets, one-off automation and provider-specific implementation.

Architecture is only part of the problem.

The portfolio treats product definition, policy, evidence, human authority, portability, customer control and adoption surfaces as first-class engineering concerns. Its design reflects experience across enterprise cloud architecture, platform strategy, product management and modernization.

IaaS is what we buy.
Infrastructure-as-a-Product is what we build.
