ABOUT THE PORTFOLIO

Built from the gap between cloud infrastructure and
product engineering.

Infrastructure Product Works is an independent practitioner portfolio exploring a simple proposition: organizations should be able to consume governed infrastructure as durable products rather than repeatedly rebuilding architecture through tickets, one-off automation and provider-specific implementation.

CLEAR BOUNDARY

IPW is evidence of experience.

Infrastructure Product Works demonstrates Larry Cureton's experience, thinking, architecture, software proof and evidence discipline. It is not an official government product, not an official agency or department system, not a procurement proposal and not a request that any agency adopt, buy, license or operate a private product.

WHY THIS PORTFOLIO EXISTS

The problem is bigger than provisioning.

Organizations can buy capable cloud services and still struggle to turn them into dependable internal products. The missing work sits across architecture, product management, engineering, governance, evidence and operating-model design.

PLATFORM STRATEGY

Start with the consumer boundary.

Define the governed outcome, supported choices and lifecycle before exposing implementation topology.

PRODUCT MANAGEMENT

Treat adoption as part of the product.

Usability, reversibility, documentation, lifecycle and clear ownership matter just as much as the infrastructure machinery behind the experience.

ARCHITECTURE + ENGINEERING

Keep authority and evidence visible.

AI, validation, human judgment and reconciliation have different responsibilities. The architecture should preserve those boundaries and make important claims testable.

GOVERNANCE

Evidence over assertion.

Architecture and maturity claims should trace to versioned contracts, tests, revisions and retained proof rather than presentation alone.

MODERNIZATION

Build the platform foundation with the change.

Modern applications need repeatable platform capability, not another generation of bespoke infrastructure hidden behind new tooling.

CROSS-DOMAIN LEADERSHIP

The work spans disciplines on purpose.

The portfolio combines product thinking, architecture, engineering, platform strategy and regulated-enterprise modernization because infrastructure productization requires those disciplines to work together.

IaaS is what organizations buy. Infrastructure-as-product is what they build.

That distinction is the thread connecting the portfolio's product, architecture and engineering work.

THE EXPERIENCE BEHIND THE PORTFOLIO

Enterprise architecture meets product thinking.

The portfolio reflects work across enterprise cloud architecture, platform strategy, modernization and product management in large, regulated environments. Its point of view comes from seeing the same pattern repeatedly: organizations can purchase capable cloud infrastructure and still struggle to turn it into dependable internal capability.

The public thesis explains the model. Storefront makes the consumer boundary tangible. Guard makes architecture and evidence testable. Forge explores governed product construction. Console preserves human review. Assurance carries authority and safeguards forward. Crossplane describes the reconciliation boundary behind the product contract.
