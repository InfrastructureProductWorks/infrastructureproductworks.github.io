SHARED INTELLIGENCE · BOUNDED AUTHORITY

One Composite AI service.
Authority stays outside the model.

Composite AI is a shared, provider-neutral capability used by Infrastructure Product Works applications. It is not another user-facing product. It interprets, proposes, recommends, composes, explains and assembles evidence through bounded contracts while deterministic controls and accountable people retain authority.

CONSUMER MODEL

Shared intelligence without five different AI stacks.

STOREFRONT

Interpret intent

Help translate consumer intent and explain bounded product choices without changing the product contract or entitlement rules.

FORGE

Recommend composition

Draft product compositions and artifacts while inherited constraints, publication and approval remain governed outside AI.

CONSOLE

Explain evidence

Summarize verified evidence, findings and possible next actions without inheriting decision authority.

GUARD

Propose remediation

Explain deterministic findings and suggest bounded remediation while Guard remains the source of the deterministic validation result.

ASSURANCE

Assist interpretation

Help explain evidence packages while authority, custody, scope, safeguards and provenance remain deterministically verified.

AI proposes. IPW verifies.
Accountable authority decides.

Automation executes only within approved bounds. A model cannot approve its own recommendation, expand its tools, weaken trusted policy or become a shortcut around Guard, Assurance or required human review.

DOCUMENTATION-FIRST GATE

Define the contract before expanding the runtime.

CONTRACT

Common request and response envelopes

Consumer, operation, context, scope, source revisions, permitted capabilities, structured results, digests and trace identifiers travel through one shared contract.

PROVIDER

Models remain replaceable

Approved model providers sit behind the service boundary. Replacing a provider must not redefine an IPW product contract or silently expand authority.

PROVENANCE

AI output leaves a trace

Requests, source references, model identity where applicable, result digests, downstream validations and required decisions remain traceable.

FAILURE

Fail boundedly

Malformed output, unavailable models, provenance failures, stale context or attempted authority expansion produce explicit failure states rather than weaker fallback behavior.

FIRST IMPLEMENTATION PROOF

Synthetic request → structured AI response → deterministic verification → human-review boundary → evidence package.

No live cloud, production credential, privileged execution or live-model dependency is required to prove the architecture.
