PORTFOLIO USE NOTICE

Use the public portfolio with authority boundaries intact.

This notice describes the current public portfolio, synthetic demos, repository evidence and bounded engineering surfaces.

Public portfolio boundaries

Intended use

IaaP Guard is presented as a bounded validation surface for infrastructure pull-request workflows. It is intended to demonstrate deterministic findings and reviewable evidence that support, rather than replace, organization-owned decision processes.

No deployment authorization

A PASS, WARNING, FAIL, report, recommendation or other Guard output does not constitute approval, apply, production deployment, privileged access or authorization to bypass customer controls.

Customer responsibility

Customers remain responsible for repository administration, branch protections, approvals, credentials, infrastructure configuration, deployment decisions, regulatory obligations and the suitability of Guard outputs for their environment.

Beta status

Public portfolio functionality may change as the work is validated and improved. Pages, demos, repositories and examples may be modified, suspended or withdrawn.

Third-party services

Guard depends on GitHub services for its GitHub App and repository workflow integration. Use of GitHub remains subject to GitHub's own terms, availability and controls.

Acceptable use

Do not use the service to violate law, compromise systems, evade access controls, interfere with other users or misrepresent Guard validation as independent production authorization.

Intellectual property

Infrastructure Product Works and IaaP Guard names, product materials and proprietary components remain subject to their applicable ownership and license terms. Public availability does not grant rights beyond an applicable license or written agreement.

No warranty

Public portfolio surfaces are provided as available and without warranty of uninterrupted operation, fitness for a particular purpose or error-free validation. Any implementing organization should independently review infrastructure changes before deployment.

Changes and termination

Access may be limited or terminated for misuse, security concerns, legal requirements or discontinuation of a public portfolio surface. This notice may be updated as the portfolio and evidence model mature.
