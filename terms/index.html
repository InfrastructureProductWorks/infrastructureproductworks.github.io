PORTFOLIO USE NOTICE

Public demos and repository evidence with
clear authority boundaries.

This notice explains the boundary for public access to Infrastructure Product Works portfolio pages, synthetic demos, repository evidence and public engineering surfaces.

NOT A COMMERCIAL OFFER

IPW is a portfolio, not a procurement channel.

Infrastructure Product Works is an independent practitioner portfolio and evidence site. It is not an official government product, not an official agency or department system, not a procurement proposal, not a request for adoption, not production software and not a commercial offer through this website.

WHY THE BOUNDARY MATTERS

The portfolio is easiest to understand when evidence, demonstration and authority are kept separate.

Infrastructure Product Works deliberately shows working concepts, synthetic demos and engineering evidence without presenting those things as production authority. That distinction lets visitors evaluate what has actually been demonstrated while keeping procurement, deployment, risk acceptance and official organizational decisions in their proper channels.

DEMONSTRATION

Shows how a capability could work.

Public surfaces make product and architecture ideas tangible with synthetic data and bounded behavior.

EVIDENCE

Shows what has been built or validated.

Repositories, tests, contracts and retained artifacts support claims about engineering progress and maturity.

AUTHORITY

Remains organization-owned.

Approval, production deployment, procurement, risk acceptance and operational use remain outside the authority of the public portfolio.

Public demo boundaries

Intended use

The public pages and demos are provided for portfolio review, concept inspection, documentation feedback and engineering-evidence review.

No deployment authorization

A PASS, WARNING, FAIL, report, recommendation or other output does not constitute approval, apply, production deployment, privileged access, ATO, risk acceptance or authorization to bypass organization-owned controls.

Organization responsibility

Any evaluating or implementing organization remains responsible for its own repositories, approvals, credentials, infrastructure, regulatory obligations and suitability decisions.

Synthetic data only

Public demos use synthetic data and are not designed to receive secrets, customer data, taxpayer data, protected health data, production infrastructure details or proprietary environment information.

Third-party services

Some public surfaces may link to GitHub, Render or other external hosting surfaces. Use of those services remains subject to their own terms, availability and controls.

Acceptable use

Do not use the public portfolio surfaces to violate law, compromise systems, evade access controls, interfere with other visitors or misrepresent portfolio evidence as operational authorization.

Intellectual property

Infrastructure Product Works names, materials and component concepts identify the independent portfolio. Public availability does not grant rights beyond an applicable license or written permission.

No warranty

Public portfolio surfaces are provided as available and without warranty of uninterrupted operation, fitness for a particular purpose or error-free validation.

Future changes

Pages, demos, repositories and examples may change as the portfolio and evidence model mature.

PUBLIC-SECTOR USE

Official use should be official and organization-owned.

Any government use of related concepts should be developed through official channels as government-owned work with appropriate ethics, procurement and management guidance. The website itself is not the adoption path.
