INDEPENDENT PRACTITIONER PORTFOLIO

Infrastructure-as-product,
shown as working evidence.

IaaS is what organizations buy; infrastructure-as-product is the operating model they build.

Infrastructure Product Works is Larry Cureton's independent evidence portfolio. It demonstrates architecture, product thinking, bounded public demos and engineering proof for turning raw infrastructure into governed, reusable platform capability.

PROVISIONING VS PRODUCTIZATION

Provisioning creates infrastructure. Productization creates a governed way to deliver it.

The portfolio demonstrates a system model for productizing infrastructure provisioning, governance, consumption and evidence-bounded delivery. The model reduces how much specialized implementation knowledge must be carried at the point of consumption.

TRADITIONAL PROVISIONING

The engineer carries the complexity.

Requests move through tickets, handoffs, provider-specific settings, policy interpretation and one-off code. Outcomes depend heavily on each engineer knowing the right details at the right time.

PRODUCTIZED PLATFORM CAPABILITY

The platform carries more of the complexity.

A team asks for a governed outcome. The model constrains choices, validates requirements, preserves evidence and hands the request to an authorized organization-owned fulfillment path.

ENGINEERING PROOF

The claim is inspectable.

Public repositories, bounded demos, tests, contracts and maturity notes show what has been demonstrated and what remains validation-only.

STANDARD + EXCEPTION PATHS

Standard work is automated. Exceptional work is engineered. All work stays governed.

A product operating model should not collapse when a request falls outside the catalog. The IPW model keeps the request, decision context, validation and evidence inside the governed lifecycle while specialized engineers handle the portion that cannot yet be fulfilled through a standard product.

STANDARD PATH

Automate the known path.

Approved products carry bounded choices, entitlements, validation and reusable delivery behavior so routine consumption does not require one-off infrastructure engineering.

EXCEPTION PATH

Escape from automation, not from governance.

When specialized engineering is required, fulfillment can be delegated to the appropriate engineering team without turning the request into a parallel, untracked delivery process.

PRODUCT DISCOVERY

Turn recurring exceptions into products.

Repeated non-standard requests become signals for the platform roadmap: candidate patterns can be reviewed, bounded, validated and promoted into reusable infrastructure products.

Operating principle: IPW does not attempt to eliminate infrastructure engineering. The model puts infrastructure engineering inside a governed product operating model, so non-standard work can remain visible, reviewable and capable of becoming tomorrow's standard path.

PROBLEMS THE PORTFOLIO EXPLORES

Modern infrastructure work often breaks between intent and execution.

 CONSOLE

Console demonstrates how verified findings, exceptions and decision context can be reviewed without granting approval authority.

 FORGE

Patterns get rebuilt.

Forge demonstrates how governed product proposals and lifecycle artifacts can preserve reusable intent.

 ASSURANCE

Authority can drift.

Assurance demonstrates how scope, custody, purpose and evidence can remain bound as technical action moves downstream.

 CROSSPLANE

Execution must stay bounded.

Crossplane is presented as an architectural reconciliation boundary for approved claims, not as proof of operational deployment.
