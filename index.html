THE GOVERNED LIFECYCLE

One portfolio. Bounded responsibilities.

This is a responsibility map, not a claim that every deployment must execute every application in one rigid linear chain. The important thing is that each boundary remains explicit and evidence can be carried across it.

EXPERIENCE

Storefront

Capture a bounded infrastructure-product order through a stable consumer contract.

VALIDATE

Guard

Evaluate architecture, security, policy and evidence deterministically.

REVIEW

Console

Present verified evidence and context to customer-controlled human review.

CONSTRUCT

Forge

Normalize accepted intent and evidence into governed product proposals and artifacts.

ASSURE

Assurance

Verify that delegated authority, scope, custody and safeguards remain valid.

RECONCILE

Crossplane

Continuously reconcile authorized product state through replaceable providers.

Human authorization is a boundary, not a hidden seventh application.

Guard findings do not approve. Console visibility does not approve. Forge proposals do not approve. Assurance verifies bounded authority. Crossplane reconciliation begins only after the applicable authorization boundary has been satisfied.

BEHIND THE COUNTER

Simple outside. Engineered inside.

The consumer experience is intentionally simple. Behind it, Storefront, Guard, Forge, Console, Assurance, governance, stable product contracts, Crossplane and cloud providers each have bounded responsibilities.

Storefront is the experience. Contracts are the boundary.
Authorized people decide.

Composite AI may interpret intent, propose changes, explain policy and assemble evidence. Deterministic controls decide what is admissible. Human authority remains explicit. Crossplane reconciles authorized product state.

THE REFERENCE ARCHITECTURE

Build the product boundary first.

Experience stays replaceable. Intelligence stays bounded. Governance stays testable. The product contract stays stable. The control plane remains authoritative. Provider topology stays behind the boundary.

PORTABLE BY EVIDENCE, NOT BY ASSERTION

Governed change leaves a trail.

Change → validation → approval → reconciliation → status → evidence. Portability comes from stable contracts, replaceable implementations and retained evidence—not simply from calling an architecture multi-cloud.

A working portfolio, not a slide-deck architecture.
