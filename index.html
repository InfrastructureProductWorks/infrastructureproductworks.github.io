THE PRODUCT PORTFOLIO

Purpose-built surfaces around one governed lifecycle.

VALIDATE

IaaP Guard™

GitHub-native deterministic architecture and evidence validation. Guard asks whether infrastructure is actually being designed, delivered and governed as a product.

DEFINE & RENDER

IaaP Forge™

Consumes frozen Guard evidence and turns approved intent into stable infrastructure-product proposals and lifecycle artifacts.

DECIDE

IaaP Console™

Evidence, traceability and human-selection surfaces that preserve the boundary between validated evidence and authorized intent.

ASSURE

IaaP Assurance™

Authority, custody, safeguard-continuity, rollback and continuous-assurance evidence across the governed lifecycle.

DISCOVER

Storefront

First-class browse, configure, order, track and handoff-inspection experience over the stable product-order contract.

CONTROL PLANE

Crossplane

Reconciles approved infrastructure-product claims while provider-specific implementations remain behind stable contracts.

PORTABLE BY EVIDENCE, NOT BY ASSERTION

Governed change leaves a trail.

Change → validation → approval → reconciliation → status → evidence. Provider-specific implementations can span AWS, Azure, GCP or approved on-premises infrastructure without making a provider tool the consumer boundary.
