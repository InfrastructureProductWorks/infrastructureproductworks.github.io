INFRASTRUCTURE-AS-A-PRODUCT

Cloud resources are ingredients.
Developers need products.

IaaS is what we buy; infrastructure-as-a-product is what we build.

Nobody pulls into a drive-thru hungry for hot, golden-brown french fries and orders a potato. The potato matters. So do the preparation, cooking, seasoning, packaging and quality controls. But those are implementation details behind the product the customer actually wants. Cloud infrastructure should work the same way.

Place the infrastructure orderSee behind the counter

THE INGREDIENTS

Raw cloud resources

Networks, subnets, IAM, clusters, routing, DNS, encryption, provider APIs and implementation code all matter. They are the potatoes, oil, heat and process behind the counter.

→

THE PRODUCT

Cloud Foundation Environment

A developer asks for the governed outcome they need. The platform owns how approved ingredients become that product.

Infrastructure-as-Code can automate the recipe. Infrastructure-as-a-Product defines the menu, product contract, ordering experience, quality controls and lifecycle around it.

BEHIND THE COUNTER

The product looks simple because the platform absorbs the complexity.

Now take the wall off the kitchen. Forge, Guard, governance, product contracts, Crossplane and cloud providers do different jobs behind the consumer boundary.

Developers order the outcome—not the ingredients.

Storefront is the experience. Contracts are the boundary.
Authorized people decide.

Composite AI may interpret intent, propose changes, explain policy and assemble evidence. Deterministic controls decide what is valid. Crossplane reconciles authorized product state.

THE REFERENCE ARCHITECTURE

Build the product boundary first.

Experience is replaceable. Intelligence is bounded. Governance is testable. The contract is stable. The control plane is authoritative.

MEASURE WHAT DEVELOPERS EXPERIENCE

Activity is not the product.

Lines of infrastructure code, modules built and tickets closed are engineering activity. Product value shows up at the consumer boundary: provisioning speed, product health, diagnosis, restoration, adoption, exception rate and developer experience.

Measure the developer experience—not the activity behind it.

THE PRODUCT PORTFOLIO

Different stations. One governed lifecycle.

EXPERIENCE

Storefront

Browse, configure, order, track and inspect handoff through the stable InfrastructureProductOrder contract.

VALIDATE

IaaP Guard™

Deterministically asks whether infrastructure is being designed, delivered and governed as a product.

DEFINE & RENDER

IaaP Forge™

Consumes frozen evidence and turns approved intent into stable infrastructure-product proposals and lifecycle artifacts.

DECIDE

IaaP Console™

Presents evidence, traceability and human-selection surfaces without turning evidence into authority.

ASSURE

IaaP Assurance™

Preserves authority, custody, safeguard continuity, rollback and assurance evidence across the lifecycle.

RECONCILE

Crossplane

Reconciles authorized infrastructure-product claims while provider implementations stay behind stable contracts.

GUARD THE PRODUCT BOUNDARY

How do you know the kitchen is still making the product correctly?

IaaP Guard evaluates architecture and evidence in GitHub. It validates; it does not provision infrastructure or turn a finding into production authority.

PORTABLE BY EVIDENCE, NOT BY ASSERTION

Governed change leaves a trail.

Change → validation → approval → reconciliation → status → evidence. Portability comes from replaceable provider implementations, stable contracts and retained evidence—not simply from saying a workload is multi-cloud.
