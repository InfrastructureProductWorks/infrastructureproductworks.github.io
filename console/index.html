DECIDE · IAAP CONSOLE

Make evidence understandable
without turning it into authority.

IaaP Console is a customer-hosted review experience for verified Guard evidence, Forge product visibility, traceability and human workbenches. It consumes versioned producer contracts rather than quietly reimplementing their logic.

YOU PROBABLY NEED CONSOLE IF...

Your review evidence exists, but nobody has one trustworthy place to understand it.

TOOL SPRAWL

Findings and decisions are scattered everywhere.

Reviewers chase pull requests, artifacts, repositories and spreadsheets just to reconstruct the context around one infrastructure change.

PROVENANCE

People can see a report but cannot prove where it came from.

Console verifies producer revision and digest before presenting Guard or Forge material so the review surface remains tied to its source.

REVIEW FRICTION

Architecture, security and audit teams each need a different view.

Search, filtering, traceability and review workbenches help each audience focus on relevant evidence without creating separate copies of the truth.

AUTHORITY CONFUSION

A dashboard starts behaving like a decision engine.

Console deliberately separates visibility from authority: it presents verified context while authorized people and systems retain the actual decision.

Console is the remedy: make evidence searchable, traceable and reviewable in one customer-controlled workspace without duplicating producer logic or quietly acquiring approval authority.

THE PROBLEM

Evidence scattered across tools is hard to govern.

A platform team may have findings in pull requests, product proposals in another repository, evidence digests in artifacts and human review happening somewhere else. Console creates a coherent review surface while preserving the source of truth: producers keep ownership of their verdicts and proposals, and reviewers can verify what they are looking at before acting on it.

WHAT YOU ACTUALLY GET

One place to review the evidence and product work already being produced.

Console turns Guard and Forge outputs into a customer-controlled review workspace. It helps people find, verify and understand the material without changing the underlying verdicts or product proposals.

GUARD VIEWS

Findings and planning reports in one workbench

Review Guard results, findings, generated objectives, key results, Epics and supporting planning details without digging through separate artifacts or pull-request logs.

FORGE VIEWS

Product catalog and lifecycle visibility

See Forge product definitions, proposal context, lifecycle state and supporting evidence so reviewers understand what product is being discussed and where it stands.

SEARCH & FILTER

Find the evidence that matters

Search, filter and inspect findings, planning output and product context so architecture, security, product and audit users can focus on the material relevant to them.

TRACEABILITY

See where every result came from

Console retains producer revision, digest and evidence lineage so a reviewer can trace what is displayed back to the Guard or Forge material that produced it.

REVIEW WORKBENCH

Notes, markers and review queues

Human reviewers get a workspace for review context, materiality, notes and bounded workflow markers without Console silently turning those aids into approval authority.

EXPORT

Evidence and traceability packages

Generate bounded manifests and review-ready exports that retain source history for audits, architecture reviews, governance discussions and handoffs.

Console is the place to understand and review the work. It does not rewrite Guard findings, change Forge proposals, approve infrastructure or become a second control plane.

SIGNATURE REVIEW MODEL

Verified evidence. Human review.

Console binds what a reviewer sees back to pinned producer evidence, presents it through a customer-controlled workbench, and keeps the actual decision outside Console's authority.

DESIGN DECISIONS

Keep review useful without creating another control plane.

PROVENANCE

Verify before display

Imported material remains bound to producer revision and digest so the workbench cannot silently substitute different evidence.

SEPARATION

Don't duplicate producers

Guard remains responsible for Guard verdicts. Forge remains responsible for Forge product proposals. Console consumes their contracts.

HUMAN REVIEW

Context, not inferred authority

The workbench can surface materiality, notes and review context without inventing hidden priority or decision rights.

TRACEABILITY

Export without erasure

Bounded manifests can be exported while retaining the original source history and evidence lineage.

A workbench for decisions.
Not a decision engine.

The point of Console is to make governed evidence easier for people to understand without allowing presentation logic to become approval, provisioning or reconciliation authority.

CURRENT MATURITY

Customer-hosted. Synthetic validation. Non-authoritative.

The current Console baseline remains pre-1.0 and under CONTINUE_VALIDATION. Live adapters, customer-data processing, provisioning, lifecycle decisions and commercial activation remain separately governed.
