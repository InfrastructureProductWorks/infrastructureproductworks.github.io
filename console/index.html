DECIDE · IAAP CONSOLE

Make evidence understandable
without turning it into authority.

IaaP Console is a customer-hosted modular experience for verified Guard evidence, Forge visibility, traceability, review workbenches and bounded administration views. It consumes versioned contracts rather than duplicating Guard or Forge logic.

HUMAN REVIEW WORKBENCH

Console keeps source evidence and human review context together without becoming the producer, approver or reconciler.

SEPARATION OF DUTIES

Visibility is not authority.

Guard owns its verdicts. Forge owns its product proposals. Console presents verified projections and customer-controlled review surfaces without silently inheriting either component's authority.

A workbench for decisions.
Not a decision engine.

Console can present verified evidence, review markers, draft notes and product visibility. It does not approve, provision, reconcile, infer hidden hierarchy or override producer verdicts.

CURRENT POSTURE

Customer-hosted. Synthetic validation. Non-authoritative.

The current Console baseline remains pre-1.0 and under CONTINUE_VALIDATION. It does not authorize live adapters, customer-data processing, provisioning, lifecycle decisions or commercial activation.
