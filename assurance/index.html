ASSURE · IAAP ASSURANCE

Move approved direction toward action
without losing the safeguards.

IaaP Assurance is a documentation-first reference architecture with bounded executable proofs for translating approved mission direction into scoped, policy-aligned and evidence-producing technical action.

SIGNATURE ASSURANCE MODEL

Mission direction reaches the data plane through governed authorization.

Approved direction is bound to identity, purpose, scope, time limits and required approvals. The assurance checkpoint verifies the package before a bounded path can continue.

01

Direction

Begin with approved mission or operational direction.

02

Authority Package

Bind identity, purpose, scope, time limits and required approvals.

03

Assurance Gate

Deterministically verify that authority and constraints match the request.

04

Scoped Action

Allow the bounded path or hold and route for additional review.

05

Evidence

Retain independent evidence of the decision and resulting path.

Authorized officials retain legal, privacy, policy and mission determinations. Assurance verifies that the resulting authority package exists, remains active and matches the requested purpose and scope.

FRACTAL-RECURSIVE ASSURANCE

The same checkpoint repeats down the hierarchy.

Constraints narrow downward across enterprise, platform, infrastructure-product, resource and protected-data levels while evidence aggregates upward. This is an accepted bounded synthetic proof, not a claim of production-scale runtime authority.

MATURITY BOUNDARY

Bounded proofs, not production authority.

Assurance is documentation-first and pre-pilot. Gate 6 is accepted only for deterministic synthetic custody proof. It does not claim production authorization, unrestricted autonomous remediation, immutable cloud state or operational multi-agency readiness.
