NORTHSTAR SIGNAL · CLOUD PRODUCT STRATEGY & DECISION INTELLIGENCE

Decide what should become a product.
Keep the outcome attached.

Northstar Signal helps accountable leaders determine whether a reusable cloud capability should be reused, created, phased, deferred or redirected, then keeps that decision connected to authorization, delivery and measured benefit.

THE JOURNEY

Leadership intent stays visible all the way through delivery.

Northstar is the decision and outcome layer in the IPW operating model. It does not replace the team's backlog, deployment tooling or technical authority.

01

Outcome

Division Objective, Key Result, baseline and target.

02

Evidence

Existing products, capabilities and constraints.

03

Decision

Reuse, build, phase, defer or redirect.

04

Authorize

Bound the product intent in a CAR.

05

Handoff

Translate the authorized Epic into the team's backlog.

06

Deliver

Guard, Console, Forge, Storefront and Assurance produce evidence.

07

Measure

Compare delivery evidence with actual KR outcome evidence.

WHO IT SERVES

One journey. Different responsibilities.

LEADERSHIP

What should we authorize?

See mission consequence, alternatives, evidence confidence, investment context and exactly what the decision permits.

MANAGEMENT

How does intent become executable?

Translate the authorized outcome into Epics, dependencies, sequencing and delivery-team assignment.

DELIVERY

What must remain true while we build?

Keep strategy, CAR constraints, evidence requirements and outcome lineage attached to the work.

Closing tickets is not the same thing as achieving the outcome.

Northstar deliberately separates delivery progress from authoritative Key Result measurement. That makes it harder for activity to masquerade as benefit.
