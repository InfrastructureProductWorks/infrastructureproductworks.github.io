DEFINE & RENDER · IAAP FORGE

Turn approved intent into a
governed infrastructure product.

IaaP Forge occupies the product-construction boundary. It consumes bounded intent and accepted evidence, normalizes that material into stable product context, permits Composite AI to propose within defined limits, and subjects resulting artifacts to deterministic gates before any authorized downstream reconciliation.

THE PROBLEM

Generation without boundaries is not infrastructure product engineering.

AI can help interpret intent, assemble proposals and explain tradeoffs. But an infrastructure platform cannot let a probabilistic proposal silently become approved topology, privilege or production state. Forge separates useful intelligence from authority by putting deterministic contracts, evidence bindings and human authorization around the proposal path.

SIGNATURE PRODUCT MODEL

Forge builds the product behind the order.

Developers order the outcome. Forge converts bounded intent and immutable evidence into deterministic product proposals while human authority and the product control plane remain explicit.

DESIGN DECISIONS

Keep the product stable while implementations evolve.

EVIDENCE

Consume, don't rewrite

Accepted upstream evidence remains identifiable to its source revision rather than becoming a new untraceable assertion inside Forge.

INTELLIGENCE

AI proposes only

Composite AI can interpret, propose and explain. Deterministic controls and authorized people remain responsible for admissibility and permission.

CONTRACT

Bind the selected product

Product selection, evidence and rendered output remain explicitly bound so a different selection cannot be substituted after review.

CONTROL PLANE

Reconciliation stays downstream

Forge prepares governed product artifacts. The authoritative reconciler remains behind a separately authorized control-plane boundary.

Guard validates the change.
Forge constructs the product proposal.

The two products share evidence but not authority. That separation keeps deterministic assessment, product construction and reconciliation independently testable.

CURRENT MATURITY

Supported V1. Continued validation before wider authority.

Forge's public architecture records an evidence-based CONTINUE_VALIDATION posture: supported V1 remains bounded while later capability and external product-outcome evidence are evaluated separately. Production approval, merge, apply and privilege authority remain outside Forge.
