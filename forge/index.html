DEFINE & RENDER · IAAP FORGE

Turn approved intent into a
governed infrastructure product.

IaaP Forge occupies the product-construction boundary. It consumes bounded intent and accepted evidence, normalizes that material into stable product context, permits Composite AI to propose within defined limits, and subjects resulting artifacts to deterministic gates before any authorized downstream reconciliation.

THE PROBLEM

Generation without boundaries is not infrastructure product engineering.

AI can help interpret intent, assemble proposals and explain tradeoffs. But an infrastructure platform cannot let a probabilistic proposal silently become approved topology, privilege or production state. Forge separates useful intelligence from authority by putting deterministic contracts, evidence bindings and human authorization around the proposal path.

WHAT YOU ACTUALLY GET

Reviewable infrastructure-product artifacts, not an AI-generated black box.

Forge takes an approved product request and trusted upstream evidence and turns them into a structured proposal that engineers can review, test and hand off through normal controls.

NORMALIZED INTENT

A stable product initiative

Forge converts the selected product, supported inputs and accepted evidence into a deterministic structure so every downstream artifact refers to the same product intent.

PRODUCT CONTRACT

A versioned product definition

Teams receive a stable description of the infrastructure product: what outcome it provides, what inputs are allowed, what controls it inherits and which implementation details stay behind the boundary.

PROPOSAL

An explainable implementation proposal

Composite AI can help assemble and explain a candidate design, but its output remains a proposal tied to the selected product and evidence rather than an instruction to deploy.

RENDERED ARTIFACTS

Deterministic product bundles

Forge renders bounded product artifacts that can be validated by schemas, policy and tests and then consumed by the approved downstream fulfillment path.

GITHUB REVIEW

Human-reviewable changes

Product changes can be prepared for the normal GitHub review and merge process with source revision, evidence and selected-product bindings preserved for reviewers.

SUPPLY-CHAIN EVIDENCE

Provenance and release records

Forge preserves traceability around the product artifacts, including source and evidence bindings and the supporting provenance, lifecycle and release records needed for later verification.

PRODUCT DIRECTION · FORGE WORKBENCH

What Forge could look like when finished.

A cloud product-engineering studio where Platform, Security, Network, FinOps, Identity, Data and SRE teams turn the capabilities they own into composable infrastructure products. This is a product-direction concept, not a claim that every capability shown is implemented today.
