DEFINE & RENDER · IAAP FORGE

Turn enterprise cloud capabilities into
governed infrastructure products.

IaaP Forge is the product-engineering boundary where Platform, Security, Network, FinOps, Identity, Data and SRE teams can define the capabilities they own, compose them into stable infrastructure products, preserve evidence, and prepare reviewable product artifacts without acquiring approval or provisioning authority.

Open Forge PreviewExplore Forge capabilities

Interactive preview: synthetic demonstration data only. Forge does not approve requests, provision infrastructure or connect this preview to production environments.

YOU PROBABLY NEED FORGE IF...

Your enterprise has plenty of automation but very few actual infrastructure products.

MODULE SPRAWL

Terraform modules are being mistaken for products.

Reusable code exists, but consumers still need to understand implementation details, assemble dependencies and negotiate security, networking and operations separately.

DOMAIN SILOS

Security, Network, FinOps and SRE contribute through documents and review queues.

Forge gives those teams a way to define versioned capabilities that can become part of the product itself instead of remaining external paperwork.

MULTI-CLOUD DUPLICATION

Each cloud has become its own platform program.

Forge keeps the consumer product contract above AWS, Azure and GCP implementation choices so teams can productize the outcome once and map provider-specific behavior behind it.

LIFECYCLE GAP

Templates are published without ownership, compatibility or release evidence.

Forge treats infrastructure as a product with contracts, composition, versions, impact analysis, economics and evidence-backed lifecycle artifacts.

Forge is the remedy: turn domain-owned cloud capabilities into governed, composable infrastructure products before developers ever see the provider machinery underneath.

WHAT YOU ACTUALLY GET

A cloud product engineering studio, not another IaC editor.

PRODUCT CONTRACTS

Stable consumer outcomes

Define what a product promises, what consumers may choose, and which implementation details remain platform-owned.

COMPOSITION

Domain-owned capabilities

Compose versioned Network, Security, FinOps, SRE, Identity and provider profiles without collapsing ownership boundaries.

IMPACT

Understand change before release

See which products, providers, controls and evidence are affected when a shared capability changes.

GUARDRAILS

Deterministic validation

Bind schemas, policy, tests and Guard evidence to the product so review does not depend on memory or hidden state.

ECONOMICS

Engineer cost into the product

FinOps can contribute cost profiles, allocation requirements and bounded planning estimates before infrastructure is ordered.

RELEASES

Evidence-backed lifecycle

Track product versions, compatibility, provenance and release evidence while keeping the human release decision explicit.

ACCEPTED ENTERPRISE CONTRACT BASELINE

The productization model is now accepted on protected main.

IDENTITY & COMPOSITION

Exact product records and relationships

Forge now has an accepted documentation-first contract model for product identity, provider implementations, consumer contracts and complete composition binding.

CHANGE & LIFECYCLE

Compatibility, impact and controlled release

The accepted baseline covers compatibility, full change-impact analysis, lifecycle transitions, exceptions, economics and release evidence without letting Forge authorize itself.

EVIDENCE & TIME

Fail-closed evidence semantics

Guard evidence, provenance, trusted time, anti-replay, rotation continuity, commit revalidation and final-write controls are all explicitly bound to the exact release subject.

HUMAN AUTHORITY

Productization without autonomous execution

Forge can describe, evaluate and prepare governed product changes, but approval, provisioning, production execution, policy authority and privilege remain outside Forge.

Accepted September 14, 2026: the enterprise productization contract tranche merged as Forge PR #111 and is now part of the protected-main documentation baseline. This is contract maturity, not a claim of production execution or customer readiness.
