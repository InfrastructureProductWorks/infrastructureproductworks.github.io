Authorized product state becomes
continuously reconciled infrastructure.

Crossplane is the authoritative product control-plane technology in the reference architecture. Infrastructure Product Works does not present Crossplane as one of its applications; it is the machinery behind the product boundary that reconciles authorized claims through replaceable provider implementations.

CONTROL-PLANE MODEL

Stable product contracts in. Provider implementations behind the boundary.

The consumer asks for the product outcome. Authorized product state reaches Crossplane through a stable contract. Crossplane continuously reconciles that desired state while provider-specific topology remains implementation detail.

AUTHORITY BOUNDARY

Reconciliation begins after authorization.

Crossplane's role in this architecture is authoritative reconciliation of approved product state. It does not replace Guard validation, Forge proposal boundaries, Console review context or the human authorization model.
